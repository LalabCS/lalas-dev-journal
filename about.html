About Me.

Hi, I'm Lala.

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, concentrating in Software Engineering, while also pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Biomedical Equipment Technology at Central Piedmont Community College.

I'm an aspiring software engineer and a girl who likes to question how everything works.

My interest in technology has always gone beyond simply making something work. I want to understand why it works, what happens when it doesn't, and how it can be improved.

That curiosity initially drew me toward Software Quality Assurance (QA) and Software Development Engineering in Test (SDET). I enjoy troubleshooting, investigating problems, testing different approaches, and understanding the reasoning behind technical solutions.

As I continue exploring Computer Science, my interests have expanded into artificial intelligence, explainable AI (XAI), AI model validation, and applied AI research and engineering.

I'm particularly interested in the relationship between developing intelligent systems and evaluating their performance and reliability. I don't necessarily want to limit myself to building software or testing it. I want opportunities to explore both.

Why Two Degrees?

Pursuing Computer Science and Biomedical Equipment Technology is an intentional decision.

I enjoy programming and software development, but I'm also interested in the physical technologies that software interacts with, particularly medical equipment, electronic systems, and emerging healthcare technologies.

My goal is to explore how these fields intersect, whether through embedded software, intelligent medical devices, verification and validation, AI-enabled systems, or research and development.

I'm still discovering which direction fits me best, and that's part of what makes this journey exciting.

Why I Created This Journal

Lala's Dev Journal is my space to document that process.

I'll share my programming projects, experiments, lessons, technical questions, and the challenges I encounter along the way.

I'm currently developing a Python conversational chatbot, starting with the fundamentals and gradually exploring how to build its knowledge bank.

This journal isn't meant to showcase only polished, completed projects. It's also a record of my progress as I learn to become a better developer.

I'm learning, building, questioning, and figuring things out—one project at a time.
